NeckGuardian is a PICO Spatial app for neck wellbeing awareness and gentle posture challenges. Answer six self-report questions, balance a tower with your posture, steer a paper plane to collect stars, and record comfortable extension and side-bend angles. It includes animated setup previews, professional voice guidance, collected/missed star statistics, and the latest 30 local records. UI and speech follow the system language in Simplified Chinese or English. Real-pose activities use passthrough Full Space and return to a native window afterward. This app does not monitor posture across other apps in the background. For wellbeing reference only, not medical diagnosis or treatment. Stop if you feel discomfort.
